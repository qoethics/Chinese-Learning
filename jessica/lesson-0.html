语法点

Grammar
①能 vs. 可以 (can)

能néng means "be able to". It covers ability, or whether your situation or time allows it. It is used to ask about availability.

  • 我wǒ下午xiàwǔ三点sān diǎn能néng去qù。

    I can go at 3 pm. My schedule is free.

  • 他tā能néng游yóu一千米yìqiān mǐ。

    He can swim 1000 m. Ability.

可以kěyǐ means "be allowed to". It is used for permission, or for "it's OK to".

  • 这里zhèlǐ可以kěyǐ拍照pāizhào吗ma？

    May I take photos here? Permission.

  • 你nǐ可以kěyǐ坐zuò这儿zhèr。

    You may sit here.

Ask about time or schedule with 能néng. Ask for permission with 可以kěyǐ. For a "yes" answer, both can be used in many cases, e.g. 你nǐ能néng／可以kěyǐ来lái吗ma？
②在 + Verb = am / is / are doing

Pattern: Subject + 在 + Verb. Add time words to show past or present.

  • 你nǐ在zài做zuò什么shénme？

    What are you doing?

  • 昨天zuótiān晚上wǎnshang你nǐ在zài做zuò什么shénme？

    What were you doing last night?

③了 = completed action (past)

Pattern: Subject + Verb + 了 + Object.

  • 我wǒ看kàn了le《哈利波特Hālì Bōtè》。

    I read Harry Potter.

④给 + person + Verb (do sth. for / to someone)

  • 我wǒ给gěi妹妹mèimei打电话dǎ diànhuà。

    I call my younger sister. Literally: I "to" sister make a call.

⑤Time words before the verb; "when" uses 什么时候

Order: Subject + Time + Place + Verb.

  • 我wǒ上周一shàng zhōuyī在zài家jiā看书kàn shū。

    Last Monday, I read at home.

  • 你nǐ什么时候shénme shíhou给gěi妹妹mèimei打dǎ了le电话diànhuà？

    When did you call your sister?

⑥怎么样 = How about…?

  • 星期xīngqīX下午xiàwǔ怎么样zěnmeyàng？

    How about Xday afternoon?

⑦一般 = usually

  • 我wǒ一般yìbān晚上wǎnshang五点wǔ diǎn做饭zuòfàn。

    I usually cook at 5 pm.

3

例句

Example Sentences
约时间 Making plans

  1. 星期xīngqīX下午xiàwǔ怎么样zěnmeyàng？

    How about Xday afternoon?

  2. 你nǐ星期xīngqīX下午xiàwǔ能néng去qùxx吗ma？

    Can you go to xx on Xday afternoon?

  3. 你nǐX号hào几点jǐ diǎn能néng去qùxx？

    What time can you go to xx on the Xth?

  4. 你nǐ23号èrshísān hào几点jǐ diǎn能néng去qù车站chēzhàn？

    What time can you go to the station on the 23rd?

  5. 我wǒ23号èrshísān hào九点半jiǔ diǎn bàn能néng去qù车站chēzhàn。

    I can go to the station at 9:30 on the 23rd.

  6. 太好了tài hǎo le，那nà我wǒ九点半jiǔ diǎn bàn到dào。

    Great, then I'll arrive at 9:30.

  7. 麦克Màikè9月30号jiǔ yuè sānshí hào能néng到dào悉尼Xīní吗ma？

    Can Mike arrive in Sydney on September 30th?

  8. 麦克Màikè10月1号shí yuè yī hào到dào纽约Niǔyuē。

    Mike arrives in New York on October 1st.

  9. 太可惜了tài kěxī le，我wǒ10月1号shí yuè yī hào在zài悉尼Xīní。

    That's a shame, I'm in Sydney on October 1st.

在做什么 What are / were you doing

  1. 你nǐ在zài做zuò什么shénme？

    What are you doing?

  2. 昨天zuótiān晚上wǎnshang你nǐ在zài做zuò什么shénme？

    What were you doing last night?

  3. 昨天zuótiān早上zǎoshang我wǒ对象duìxiàng在zài学校xuéxiào。

    Yesterday morning my partner was at school.

过去与现在 Past and present activities

  1. 上周末shàng zhōumò我wǒ在zài家jiā看电视kàn diànshì。

    Last weekend I watched TV at home.

  2. 这周末zhè zhōumò我wǒ在zài家jiā看电视kàn diànshì。

    This weekend I will watch TV at home.

  3. 上周一shàng zhōuyī我wǒ在zài家jiā看书kàn shū。

    Last Monday I read at home.

  4. 上个shàng ge星期五xīngqīwǔ我wǒ在zài书店shūdiàn看书kàn shū。

    Last Friday I read at the book store.

  5. 这zhè周二zhōu'èr晚上wǎnshang我wǒ在zài家jiā睡觉shuìjiào。

    This Tuesday evening I will sleep at home.

  6. 我wǒ10点shí diǎn睡觉shuìjiào。

    I go to sleep at 10 o'clock.

打电话 Phone calls

  1. 你nǐ给gěi谁shéi打电话dǎ diànhuà？

    Who are you calling?

  2. 我wǒ下午xiàwǔ5点wǔ diǎn给gěi我wǒ的de妹妹mèimei打dǎ了le电话diànhuà。

    I called my younger sister at 5 pm.

  3. 你nǐ什么时候shénme shíhou给gěi妹妹mèimei打dǎ了le电话diànhuà？

    When did you call your sister?

  4. 我wǒ星期二xīngqī'èr给gěi妹妹mèimei打dǎ了le电话diànhuà。

    I called my sister on Tuesday.

看书 Reading

  1. 你nǐ看kàn了le什么shénme书shū？

    What book did you read?

  2. 我wǒ看kàn了le《哈利波特Hālì Bōtè》。

    I read Harry Potter.

  3. 哈利波特Hālì Bōtè好看hǎokàn吗ma？

    Is Harry Potter good?

  4. 好看hǎokàn。

    Yes, it's good.

  5. 哪nǎ本běn哈利波特Hālì Bōtè最zuì好看hǎokàn？

    Which Harry Potter is the best?

  6. 第dì3本sān běn。

    The third one.

做饭、学习、努力 Cooking, studying, working hard

  1. 你nǐ一般yìbān几点jǐ diǎn做饭zuòfàn？

    What time do you usually cook?

  2. 我wǒ一般yìbān晚上wǎnshang五点wǔ diǎn做饭zuòfàn。

    I usually cook at 5 pm.

  3. 你nǐ上个月shàng ge yuè在zài学习xuéxí什么shénme？

    What were you studying last month?

  4. 我wǒ上个月shàng ge yuè在zài学习xuéxí中文Zhōngwén。

    Last month I was studying Chinese.

  5. 我wǒ在zài努力nǔlì上班shàngbān。

    I'm working hard at my job.

  6. 我wǒ在zài努力nǔlì学习xuéxí。

    I'm studying hard.

  7. 你nǐ在zài努力nǔlì做zuò什么shénme呢ne？

    What are you working hard on?

  8. 我wǒ在zài努力nǔlì学习xuéxí。

    I'm studying hard.
